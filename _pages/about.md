## About Me

I am a researcher working on gravitation, relativistic cosmology, and exact solutions of Einstein's equations, with a particular interest in the physical interpretation of gravitational dynamics in inhomogeneous spacetimes.

My research focuses on understanding the emergence and evolution of gravitational structure using exact and semi-analytical approaches in General Relativity and modified theories of gravity.

### Current Research Directions

- **General Relativity and modified gravity**
- **Gravitational entropy and the gravitational arrow of time**
- **Inhomogeneous cosmological models**
- **Schouten–Codazzi gravity**
- **Exact solutions of Einstein's equations**
- **Primordial black holes and relativistic cosmology**

## Research Interests

- Relativistic cosmology
- Gravitational thermodynamics and entropy
- Exact and inhomogeneous solutions in General Relativity
- Modified theories of gravity
- Black-hole and primordial-black-hole models
- Geometric and covariant methods in gravitation
