## Schouten--Codazzi Gravity

My current work on **Schouten--Codazzi gravity** focuses on the structure and physical content of the field equations, their integrability conditions, and the extent to which the theory is underdetermined.

Current directions include:

- exact cosmological solutions
- radiating and Vaidya-like geometries
- matching and junction conditions
- perturbations and covariant approaches
- black-hole solutions, horizons, singularities, and no-hair questions
- algebraic classification through Ricci/Segre structure

A central goal is to understand which properties of General Relativity are preserved and which genuinely new degrees of freedom arise in this framework.

## Gravitational Entropy and the Arrow of Time

I study proposals for **gravitational entropy** in exact and inhomogeneous spacetimes, with particular attention to the gravitational arrow of time and its relation to structure formation.

Recent work includes:

- gravitational entropy in fluids with energy flux
- Petrov Type I spacetimes
- inhomogeneous cosmological models
- covariant characterization of Weyl curvature and gravitational structure
- exact solutions as laboratories for gravitational thermodynamics

## Exact and Inhomogeneous Cosmological Models

A recurring part of my research is the use of exact solutions of Einstein's equations to study nonlinear cosmological dynamics.

Models of interest include:

- Lemaître--Tolman--Bondi spacetimes
- Szekeres class I and II solutions
- non-comoving matter models
- exact solutions with non-trivial mass-energy distributions
- relativistic peculiar-velocity fields

This direction grew directly out of my MSc and PhD work on exact cosmological solutions and inhomogeneous matter distributions.

## Exact and Semi-Analytical Models for Primordial Black Holes

I am developing exact or semi-analytical General Relativity models that can be compared directly with numerical calculations of **primordial black-hole formation**.

Current candidates include:

- scalar-field collapse models
- LTB models with and without pressure
- Szekeres class I and II geometries
- exact collapse and matching constructions

The aim is to provide analytically controlled benchmarks for numerical-relativity results.

## Modified Gravity and Cosmology

My earlier work also includes exact solutions and cosmological applications in modified-gravity frameworks, including:

- $f(T,B)$ gravity
- Cotton-gravity-inspired models
- Fab-Four cosmography
- late-time cosmology and the Hubble tension

These projects connect exact geometric methods with phenomenological questions in cosmology.
