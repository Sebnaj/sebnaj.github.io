## About Me

**Understanding gravitational structure, irreversibility, and relativistic dynamics through exact and semi-analytical solutions of General Relativity.**

I am a researcher in gravitation and relativistic cosmology at the **Instituto de Ciencias Nucleares, UNAM**. My work focuses on exact and inhomogeneous solutions of Einstein's equations, gravitational entropy, and modified theories of gravity.

I am particularly interested in connecting the geometry of spacetime with physical questions such as the **gravitational arrow of time**, structure formation, relativistic fluid dynamics, black-hole physics, and primordial black-hole formation.

### Current Research Directions

- **Gravitational entropy and the gravitational arrow of time**
- **Schouten--Codazzi gravity and modified gravity**
- **Exact and inhomogeneous solutions in General Relativity**
- **Exact and semi-analytical models for primordial black holes**

## Research Interests

- Relativistic cosmology and gravitation
- Gravitational thermodynamics and entropy
- Exact solutions of Einstein's equations
- Inhomogeneous cosmological models and relativistic fluids
- Modified theories of gravity
- Black-hole and primordial-black-hole models
